FAQ

Is SwipeUp free?

Yes. Swiping, matching, chatting and games are free. SwipeUp+ adds unlimited likes, seeing who liked you and more; new members get a free 3-day trial.

Is it in the app stores?

SwipeUp opens in your phone’s browser and installs like an app with “Add to Home Screen”. Notifications work on iPhone and Android.

Who can use it?

SwipeUp is for ages 18+. Your date of birth isn’t shown, only your age.

Which languages?

Turkish, English and German.
